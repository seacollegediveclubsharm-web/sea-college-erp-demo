# Sea College Dive Club — Unified Mobile & Cloud ERP Demo

Interactive bilingual (**العربية RTL** & **English LTR**) workflow simulator and video guide demonstrating how all 6 Sea College departments work together in real time:

1. **Sales Desk, Concierge & B2B Agencies** — Unique QR Voucher (`VO.NO`), hotel zone pickup auto-fill, deposit & `REST` tracker, and automatic B2B credit-limit guard.
2. **Operations, Boat Manifest & Transfers** — Vessel assignment, Coastguard check-in sync, **Max 4 Divers Per PADI Guide** safety guard, and automated transfer supplier payables.
3. **Travco Jetty Gate, Dive Guides, Gear & Photo** — QR boarding check (`REST` settlement), onboard upsells (`Extra Intro` / `Photo`), serialized `S.C #` gear tags, and Google/Meta ROAS trigger.
4. **Cashier & Multi-Currency Treasury** — Live `EGP` / `USD` / `EUR` / `GBP` + `VISA POS` wallets, imprest custodies, and balanced double-entry FX currency swaps.
5. **Chief Accountant (Nadia) & HR Payroll** — Automated double-entry General Ledger (`0.00 EGP` Trial Balance variance), AR/AP sub-ledgers, and attendance/commission payroll.
6. **CFO (Tarek), Owners, Fleet Partners & Marketing** — Real-time branch/vessel P&L, `24-Qirat` partner shares, capitalized drydock (`الأزق`), and closed-loop marketing ROAS.

*Note: All numbers in this public demo portal are illustrative simulation figures.*
